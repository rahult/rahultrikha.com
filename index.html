03 · About

About

I’ve been making things on the internet since a command-line companion for a Rails app in 2013. These days most of my work sits at the intersection of native apps and AI-assisted development — tools that keep humans in charge while the machines do the typing.

Everything I build tends to be local-first and quiet about it: no telemetry, no seat licenses, no cloud you didn’t ask for. The rest of the time I write at Lazily Evaluated Thoughts and ship the occasional open-source project.
